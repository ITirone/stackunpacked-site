Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy covers the Stack Unpacked application (the "App"), a private tool used by the channel owner to manage the YouTube channel Stack Unpacked.

Who uses the App

Only the channel owner. The App is not offered to the public and does not collect information from visitors, viewers or any other third party.

What the App accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the App can upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel, manage the metadata of those videos, and read the channel's own analytics and revenue reports.

How data is used and stored

The access token granted by the owner is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used solely to perform the actions above. The App does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
